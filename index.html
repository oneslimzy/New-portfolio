03 / BEFORE WE BEGIN

A few answers.

Something else on your mind?
Let's talk ↗

What can we work on together?+

Brand identities, logos, graphic design, campaigns and flyers, plus motion graphics, explainers, product walkthroughs and promotional videos. AI-assisted video concepts can also be discussed as part of the brief.

How does a project start?+

Send a short introduction to your brand, what you need and your ideal timeline. We will talk through the scope, creative direction and next steps before starting.

How long will it take?+

It depends on the scope and the assets available. We will agree on a realistic schedule before the project begins, including time for feedback and revisions.

What does a project cost?+

Pricing is based on the deliverables, complexity and timeline. Share your brief and I will prepare a quote for your project.

Can you work with my existing brand?+

Yes. I can work with your identity and guidelines to create new graphics or motion that feel consistent with your brand.
